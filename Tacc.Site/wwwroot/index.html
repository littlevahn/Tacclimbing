Product Positioning

What TACC Is

TACC is a drop-applied pre-session skin-prep formula for climbers who want sharper skin feel, cleaner contact, and more controlled grip response on indoor holds.

Not Chalk

Chalk absorbs moisture. TACC is designed to improve tack, friction feel, and grip response before you climb.

Not Lotion

TACC is not a recovery moisturizer. It is a pre-session performance product for climbers who care about skin feel and contact quality.

Built for Indoor Holds

Made for plastic and fiberglass, especially board holds, slopers, pinches, and comp-style movement.

Performance Details

Contact, Controlled

Designed around skin feel, hold response, and the repeated contact demands of modern indoor climbing.

Stick Response

Supports a more consistent, responsive feel between your skin and the hold when contact angle, pressure, and body tension matter.

Fingertip Care

Helps prepare skin feel before climbing without relying on heavy residue or thick chalk buildup.

Session Reset

A quick water-only rinse clears excess oils, chalk, and hold residue for a clean, dry contact state.
