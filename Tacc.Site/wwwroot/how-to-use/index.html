Surface Lock Protocol

Prep. Rinse. Dry. Climb.

Follow the complete sequence before climbing so TACC can settle into a clean, responsive contact state.

02–03 drops / 10+ minutes / water only

Application Sequence

Nine Steps to Contact

Keep the order intact. The waiting, water-only rinse, full dry, and chalk steps are part of the process.

  1. Apply

    Apply 2–3 drops to hands before climbing.

  2. Rub In

    Rub the product over the hands until the moisture feel is gone.

  3. Allow

    Allow approximately 10+ minutes.

  4. Rinse

    Rinse excess oils from the hands using water only.

  5. No Soap

    Do not use soap during this rinse.

  6. Dry

    Dry hands fully.

  7. Chalk

    Chalk up.

  8. Climb

    Start your session.

  9. Reapply

    Reapply between attempts if needed.

During the Session

Reset the Surface

TACC can increase how much chalk and hold residue your hands collect over time. A quick water-only rinse removes the buildup and resets the skin for clean contact. Dry fully before chalking again.

FAQ

Product Questions

Direct answers about where TACC fits in an indoor climbing session.

Is TACC chalk?

No. Chalk absorbs moisture. TACC is a drop-applied formula that helps improve tack and friction feel.

Is TACC a lotion or moisturizer?

No. TACC is a pre-session skin-prep product, not a recovery product.

Will it make my hands greasy?

Not when used correctly. Rub it in, rinse excess oils off with water, dry fully, and chalk up.

Can I use it with chalk?

Yes. TACC is designed to work with chalk.

What surfaces is it for?

TACC is built specifically for plastic and fiberglass climbing holds.

When should I use it?

Before your session, and between attempts if needed.
